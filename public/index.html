NARIMATO // QUICK GUIDE

HOW TO GET HOME

  1. WHAT TO MINE The THE WAY HOME panel lists every part the ship still needs. Rare parts are marked RARE.
  2. HOW TO MINE Click or tap a triangle to charge it. Four clicks break it into four smaller cells. Keep going deeper to find more.
  3. WHERE TO DIG Ore is hidden and appears on a random click. Follow the gold NAV beacon to the best nearby ground; WHAT WE’RE CARRYING shows your spare ore.
  4. HOW TO LEAVE When every system says LOADED, the central TRY TO FLY AWAY button opens. Take it and the next world is yours.

Stuck for two minutes? A relief ship will crash onto the planet and scatter the missing ore on marked triangles.
