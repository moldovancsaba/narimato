NARIMATO v2 — CURRENT STATUS

Public playtest build. Static Pages build with browser-state fallback; the Node build (npm start) is server-authoritative.

▶ PLAY · PHASE INDEX · STATUS

IMPLEMENTED

  • Clean v2 engine: zero-dependency server, shared game rules, WebGL spherical surface.
  • Game: surface faces are collectible; 30 breaks → next planet; 4 robots collecting/respawning.
  • Camera: constant 50° FOV, steps 4.40 / 1.50 / 1.20 / 1.01 R, horizon fixed at 25%.
  • Step 4: 1.4% R rear camera arm; gamer hidden Steps 1–3, visible on surface at Step 4.
  • WASD + arrows turn/move identically in orbit and landed; walking collects faces.
  • Click collects a surface face; wheel/pinch step the camera; keys 1–4 jump steps.
  • 1280 spherical surface faces; four robots; silent HUD; planet transition at 30/30.

TESTING

  1. Open the game; wheel/pinch or 1–4 through the four steps. Step 1 has 25% space above and the planet centre at 50%; Steps 2–4 have the horizon on the 25% line.
  2. The gamer is not shown in Steps 1–3; it appears on the surface in Step 4.
  3. Click a surface face to collect. Reach 30/30 to advance the planet.
  4. At Step 4 hold WASD/arrows to walk and collect faces you cross.
  5. Four robots tick and respawn; they do not block progress.

KNOWN GAPS

  • Pointer drag is an incremental yaw/pitch solver; the approved quaternion absolute point-follow with unlimited rotation is not implemented yet.
  • Horizon row/transitions are not yet measured by an automated 25% ±1% test.
  • Step-4 local triangle visibility/density still needs visual acceptance.
  • Gamer/robots are simple markers; v2 GDS HUD and full accessibility gates are not complete.
  • Pages build is browser-state; Node build is server-authoritative.
